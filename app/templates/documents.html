{# P29 R-031: what each stage is for, in the order a file goes through them #}
  1. Upload keeps the file exactly as sent. A type that is not accepted is quarantined, never read.
  2. Read takes the text out on this machine. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  3. Confirm - a dentist checks what was read; only then is it in the record and searchable.
  4. Show to patient puts a confirmed file in the patient's portal, until it is withdrawn.
  5. Search finds confirmed files of this patient only.
Two separate decisions A file is part of this record once you confirm it. The patient sees it in their portal only after you choose Show to patient, and stops seeing it the moment you withdraw it or move it to another record.
