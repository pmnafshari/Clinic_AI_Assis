Buttons

Hover, active and :focus-visible are interactive — tab through this row to check the focus ring. Danger carries an inset ring on purpose: primary and danger sit 6/255 apart in greyscale, so hue is the only thing separating them for a viewer who cannot use it, and this is the button that deletes a record.

Loading, empty, error

Each holds the height of what it replaces, so a card does not collapse and the page does not jump as data arrives. An empty state says what will appear and why it has not yet; an error says what failed and what to do.
